# Lantern's website

`https://lantern.boreale.dev/`: the protocol's page and its specification, in the fifteen languages of Boréale's sites (English at the root, each other one in its folder: `/fr/`, `/fr-ca/`, `/de/` ... `/zh-hant/`), and the error types the specification's `type` addresses lead to (`/errors/<name>/`, in English). Every link inside it is relative, so it works at another address too.

```sh
git submodule update --init               # library/boreale-site, what Boréale's sites share
python3 tools/build_site.py build         # the pages (SITE_OFFLINE=1: without boreale.dev's versions)
python3 tools/check_site.py build         # links, anchors, catalogs, CSP, referrers, UTM tags, error types, drafts, demo, schemas
python3 tools/make_demo.py check build/demo   # the demo at Lantern Static, its trapped variant refused (tools/requirements.txt)
python3 -m http.server -d build           # then http://localhost:8000/
SITE_AXE=axe.min.js python3 library/boreale-site/tools/site_layout.py build   # phones to desktop, with axe (Playwright)
```

- **What Boréale's sites share** is [boreale-site](https://github.com/JigSawFr/boreale-site), the submodule `library/boreale-site` (followed on its `main` by Dependabot): the fifteen languages, the header's two menus (`Site.switcher()`, `Site.languages()`), the links out and their UTM tags (`Site.links_out()`), Umami's tracker (`Site.audience()`), the projects' latest versions (`latest_versions()`), the checks every site passes (`boreale_site.check`, called by `check_site.py`), the layout check (`tools/site_layout.py`) and the assets copied into `assets/` at each build (`copy_assets()`): the fonts, every project's mark (Lantern's is `marks/lantern.svg`, also the favicon), `umami.js`, `lang.js`, `boreale.css`, `boreale.js`, and the page chrome: `chrome.css` and `chrome.js` (`Site.skip()`, `Site.sky()`, `Site.header()`, `Site.footer()`: the sky and its aurora, the header, buttons, sections, cards with their glow and reveal, bands, the footer). This site keeps its own `site.css`, `site.js`, pages and texts; a file of its own under one of the library's names stops the build.
- `template.html` is every page's frame, `hero.html` the home page's hero and `home.html` the rest of it. Each `{{key}}` is a text of `i18n/<language>.json` (`en-US.json` the reference) or of boreale-site's catalogs (the menu of Boréale's sites, the language button), which this site's do not repeat: the build stops if they do. The privacy texts and the footer's notice are boreale.dev's catalogs' word for word.
- **The specification** (`spec/`) is `SPEC.md`, rendered at each build by `build_site.py` (`blocks()`, the Markdown SPEC.md is written in, with the standard library only): each heading an anchor as GitHub makes it (`#42-handshake`), each `§ 4.2` a link to its section, its contents beside it. It stays in English in every language; the page around it is translated and says so. What the page adds around the text (`site/assets/spec.js`, `spec_body()`): the text's status line (draft, date, licence, read from SPEC.md, `status_chips()`) as chips and a card for each of the three annexes under the header; the contents as one sticky `<details>` on a phone (folded, the section being read as its summary) and a column beside the text on a large screen, the entry of the section being read marked `aria-current="true"` (an `IntersectionObserver` on the headings); a search field above them, over **`spec/search.json`** (schema 1, written by `search_index()` at each build from SPEC.md: its sections, each requirement id with its sentence, the routes and error codes; English only, so only the current English text has the field, not the drafts nor the French translation); a button beside each requirement's id that copies its link (the ids stay the anchors `#L-5.2-3`); at the foot of each top-level section a link to the previous and the next one and one to the top (`#top`). The script reads every word it says from `data-*` attributes of the page (the catalogs' `spec_*` keys), builds nothing without a page to hold it, and the page reads without it (contents open, field hidden).
- **Its drafts** (`spec/0.1/` ... `spec/0.7/`): SPEC.md as each draft was published, read from git at each build (`git show <commit>:SPEC.md`, the commits in `DRAFTS` of `build_site.py`), so that a published draft never changes; the last one may stand without a commit while it is not merged (`("0.7", None, None)`): its page is then the working tree's SPEC.md, until a pull request pins the commit that merged it; the workflow checks out the whole history for it (`fetch-depth: 0`). Each one says it is superseded (or, for the current draft, that `spec/` is its text with the fixes made since), says `noindex` and stays out of the sitemap; its relative links lead to the repository at its commit. A row of chips on every specification page leads to them (`drafts_nav()`). **`spec/changes/`** says what each draft changed: its entries of SPEC.md's "Changes", then the sections it added, changed (with the words added and removed), renamed, removed and renumbered against the draft before, read from the two texts (`outline()`, `compared()`).
- **Its translations** (`site/translations/`), informative only: `SPEC.fr.md` is the French text of the current draft, shown at `/fr/spec/` and `/fr-ca/spec/` with a banner saying that only the English text is authoritative. Its header (an HTML comment) names the English commit it translates and SPEC.md's SHA-256 at that commit, its banner and its links' words; its numbered headings take the English anchors (`#42-handshake`), and an unnumbered one gives its own (`## Modifications {#changes}`). The requirement ids, code, field names, JSON and the BCP 14 keywords stay as in English. The French text is rendered with French spacing (`french_spaces()`): the space before « : », « ; », « ? », « ! » and « » », and after « «, made non-breaking, outside code. `check_site.py` warns (never fails) when SPEC.md has changed since that commit, and while it has not, checks that every requirement id is in the translation and an anchor of its page. Other languages are welcome: see CONTRIBUTING.md.
- **The demo library** (`demo/`): a static library (SPEC.md § 21) of fictional games, homebrew and cheat sets, which `tools/make_demo.py` writes at each build from `site/demo/demo.json`, and its trapped variant (`demo/trapped/`), static libraries broken on purpose for testing clients (`site/demo/README.md`). Its page, `site/demo.html`, is in every language; the trapped libraries' page is in English, `noindex`, out of the sitemap and of `robots.txt`'s reach. The workflow runs the conformance suite against both: the demo must reach Lantern Static, each trapped library must fail the checks it names. Its files carry no tracking (`check_site.py` checks it): only the pages count visits.
- **The schemas** (`schemas/v1/<name>.json`): each schema of the repository at its `$id` (`https://lantern.boreale.dev/schemas/v1/apps.json`), copied as it is at each build (`copy_schemas()`), so that a validator resolves its `$ref`s from the site. `check_site.py` fails when a `$id` has no file, a file differs from the repository's, or one carries tracking (no Umami, no UTM tag: they are data, not pages).
- **Releases** (`releases/`, in English, linked from the footer): how each version of the text is released (SPEC.md § 3.6: a tag `spec-v<version>`, its files, a zip, `SHA256SUMS`, build provenance attestations) and how to verify one with `gh attestation verify`.
- **The error types** (`errors/`): the table of SPEC.md's section "Errors" (§ 12 in 0.3), read from it, one row and one page per `code`, at the address its `type` gives (`https://lantern.boreale.dev/errors/account-frozen`). `check_site.py` fails when one of SPEC.md's error addresses has no page.
- The look is Boréale's (`branding/README.md` in JigSawFr/boreale): the night, the aurora once at the top, Sora, Figtree and JetBrains Mono served with the site (boreale-site's `assets/fonts/`, SIL Open Font License; the language menu's flags CC BY 4.0). Lantern's mark (the frame in the aurora's colours, a lantern in snow, its flame lit) is boreale-site's `assets/marks/lantern.svg`, also the favicon; `social-preview.html` is the source of `assets/social-preview.png`, taken with Chromium at 1280 × 640 from `site/` (it reads the fonts and marks from the submodule).
- **Requirement ids**: each `[L-5.2-3]` of SPEC.md is an anchor of the specification's page (`inline()`), which the traceability page links to.
- **Conformance** (SPEC.md § 17), in English like the specification: `conformance/` (the levels, read from § 17's table), `requirements/` (each requirement, from `tools/requirements.py`, and the checks of `conformance/run.py` that cover it, or why the suite cannot see it), `implementations/` (the registry, `registry.json`, schema 1: an implementation joins it by a pull request with the suite's report) and `badge/` (the "Lantern compatible" badges, one per level, written by `badge_svg()` into `assets/badges/lantern-<draft>-<level>.svg`, after Boréale's brand: the frame in the aurora's colours around the night, the lantern in snow). The levels' table carries each level's badge, and the registry page a level-by-level table of what each implementation reaches. The home page's "Conformance" section links to them, and so does the footer. `check_site.py` checks that every requirement has its anchors, the registry its fields, every level its badge.
- **The home page**, in PlayGuard's and PlayShelf's look: under the header, the aurora (`chrome.js` draws it on `canvas#sky`, still when less motion is asked; a gradient without script) and the hero, its text beside the flow the protocol carries (the server, Lantern's versioned JSON with each file's SHA-256, the consoles and apps), drawn in snow in HTML and inline SVG so that its words are the catalog's, one above the other on a phone, its wires moving only where motion is welcome. Under the hero a request anyone can copy (`curl` on `/.well-known/lantern` of a placeholder host, with an excerpt of the handshake of SPEC.md § 4.2; the Copy button is `site.js`'s, hidden until the script runs), then what it carries (cards), how it works (four steps beside a handshake and a page of the library), what makes it solid (six cards for what the drafts 0.4 and 0.5 added, each saying since which draft, then the points that were always so; the lede points to `spec/changes/`, and so does the link beside the draft chip in the hero), what comes with the text (the specification, schemas, suite, vectors, reference server, lantern-sdk), conformance, who implements it, the demo library, and one closing: the call to read and tell us, then three short columns (what it is not, the licences, privacy; `#own`, `#licences` and `#privacy` keep their anchors).
- **Breadcrumbs** (`crumbs_html()`): a `nav` with its label (`crumbs_label`) on every page below the home page, the page itself `aria-current="page"`, in the page's language (the specification and the demo in all fifteen; the pages that are in English only use the English catalog). `check_site.py` checks it, and that the home pages have none.
- **The demo's page** puts its address first, in a read-only field with a Copy button, and a QR code of it (`tools/qr.py`, pure Python: byte mode, level M, versions 1 to 10, an inline SVG with its text alternative; `tests/test_qr.py` holds its known answer). What it holds is three cards whose counts `demo_stats()` reads from `site/demo/demo.json` at each build. The cards light up under the pointer and the sections rise into view once (`chrome.js`, not with less motion asked).
- **The header**, PlayGuard's: the page's links (how it works, the specification, conformance), "Try it" lit by the aurora to the demo library, GitHub, then the languages; up to 1080px wide, the links on a row of their own that scrolls, "Try it" first, GitHub left to the footer. The name opens the menu of Boréale's sites (boreale-site's `Site.switcher()` and `sites.json`), each with its latest release from `https://boreale.dev/versions.json` (`latest_versions()`, schema 1; another schema shows no versions). The languages' menu leads to the same page in another language, and the choice is kept (boreale-site's `assets/lang.js`, `boreale-lang`). `assets/lang-key.js` moves a choice kept under this site's former key, `lantern-lang`, there once, and is to be deleted after 2027-01-31.
- **Links out** (boreale-site's `Site.links_out()`): every page says `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">`; a link to another site has `rel="noreferrer"`, one to boreale.dev, its subdomains or github.com/JigSawFr keeps the origin (`referrerpolicy="strict-origin"`). A link to another of our sites carries `utm_source=lantern` and `utm_medium=nav`, `footer` or `link` (`Site.utm()`), one to this site or anyone else none.
- **The audience**, counted by Umami Cloud: `assets/umami.js` is its tracker, served with the site (boreale-site's copy of <https://cloud.umami.is/script.js>, `Site.audience()`), which sends each page seen to `gateway.umami.is`, without cookie, and only from `lantern.boreale.dev` (`data-domains`), not from a preview or a local copy. Its site in Umami is Boréale's, shared with the other projects' sites. The home page's privacy section says what it counts, and links to boreale.dev's privacy and legal notice.
- The Content-Security-Policy allows nothing but the site itself: no inline script or style, no font or script from elsewhere; the one address a page may send to is Umami's (`connect-src`).
- No e-mail address anywhere: contact goes through the repository's issues (`check_site.py` checks it).

`.github/workflows/site.yml` checks the submodule out (`submodules: true`), builds and checks the site for each pull request touching it (a move of `library/boreale-site` included), and publishes main's on GitHub Pages (*Settings › Pages › Source: GitHub Actions*; the custom domain is written in `build/CNAME` too).
